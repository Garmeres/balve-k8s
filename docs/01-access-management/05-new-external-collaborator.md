# New external collaborator

An external collaborator is someone outside the board who you want to give access to Balve. They do not get a garmeres email or board-level file access.

1. [Create a Balve (Nextcloud) account](09-manage-balve-users.md#add-a-new-user) for them. They will be in the **Everyone** group by default. This means they cannot access the **Garmeres board's internal files** share, but they can access the **Shared files for all Balve users** share, as well as any personal files that are shared with them directly
2. If they want to author on the website, [invite them to Strapi](11-manage-strapi-users.md#invite-a-new-user) with the **Author** role. This lets them create and edit their own content, but not other people's
