# Manage email users

All email is managed through [Domeneshop](https://domene.shop/login). Log in with the **admin@garmeres.com** credentials (found in the shared Nextcloud Passwords vault).

> **Note:** The plan has a limit of 20 email addresses.

---

## Create a new email

When someone new joins the board, create an email address for them (e.g. `fornavn@garmeres.com`):

1. Log in to [Domeneshop](https://domene.shop/login)
2. Go to **Mine domener** → **garmeres.com** → **Epost**
3. Click **Ny e-postkonto**
4. Enter the address (e.g. `fornavn`) and generate a password
5. Click **Opprett**

Then share the email credentials with the person via Nextcloud Passwords — see [Share a password with a user](07-manage-nextcloud-user.md#share-a-password-with-a-user).

---

## Add an email to a forwarding group

All board members should receive mail sent to **meile@garmeres.com**. The chairman, deputy chairman, technical manager, and other elevated roles should also receive mail from **admin@garmeres.com**.

1. Log in to [Domeneshop](https://domene.shop/login)
2. Go to **Mine domener** → **garmeres.com** → **Epost**
3. Find the forwarding address (e.g. `meile@garmeres.com` or `admin@garmeres.com`) and click it
4. Click **Legg til flere mottakere**
5. Enter the person's `@garmeres.com` address
6. Click **Legg til**

---

## Remove an email from a forwarding group

Use this when someone leaves a role but keeps their email address.

1. Log in to [Domeneshop](https://domene.shop/login)
2. Go to **Mine domener** → **garmeres.com** → **Epost**
3. Find the forwarding address and click it
4. Find the person's address in the list of recipients
5. Click the **x** next to it to remove them

---

## Remove an email

Use this when someone leaves the board and no longer needs their email. Remove unused addresses to stay within the 20-address limit.

1. Log in to [Domeneshop](https://domene.shop/login)
2. Go to **Mine domener** → **garmeres.com** → **Epost**
3. Find the email address
4. Click the **x** or **Slett** button to delete it

> **Note:** This permanently deletes the email account and any mail in it. Make sure the person has saved anything they need before deleting.

---

## Reset an email user password

If someone has forgotten their email password:

1. Log in to [Domeneshop](https://domene.shop/login)
2. Go to **Mine domener** → **garmeres.com** → **Epost**
3. Find the email address and click it
4. Enter a new password and save
5. Share the new password with the person via Nextcloud Passwords — see [Share a password with a user](07-manage-nextcloud-user.md#share-a-password-with-a-user)
