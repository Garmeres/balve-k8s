# Outgoing external collaborator

External collaborators only have a Balve account (and possibly a Strapi account). They have no email or board-level access to clean up.

1. Their Balve account can remain — as an **Everyone** user they only have access to the public shared folder and files shared with them directly. If you absolutely need to cut their access, [delete it](09-manage-balve-users.md#remove-a-user). Note that deleting permanently removes their personal files on Balve
2. If they have a Strapi account and are no longer to author the website, [delete their Strapi account](11-manage-strapi-users.md#remove-a-user)
